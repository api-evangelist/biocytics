# BioCytics

> BioCytics, Inc. is a privately held biotechnology company in Huntersville, North Carolina, founded
> in 2005 by John Powderly, MD, CPI, and clinically incubated by the co-located Carolina BioOncology
> Institute cancer research clinic. It operates a Human Applications Laboratory, a patient- and
> industry-driven BioBank, and a BioInformatics group, and sells cGMP cell-therapy manufacturing
> (CDMO), early-phase CRO services, clinical lab diagnostics and translational analytical services
> alongside its own autologous adaptive immune cell therapy pipeline for solid tumors.

## What an agent can and cannot do here

- **BioCytics publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, no API reference, no API keys
  and no machine-readable contract of any kind — no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf or WSDL
  on any host BioCytics operates.
- **There is no MCP server and no A2A agent card.** `/.well-known/agent-card.json` and
  `/.well-known/agent.json` both return 404 on biocytics.com.
- **No /.well-known/ document is served at all** — security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog and ai-plugin.json each return the site's WordPress 404.
- The only machine-readable surface on the host is WordPress's own unadvertised `/wp-json/` index
  (HTTP 200, 18 namespaces, 417 routes). That is the CMS default, its contract belongs to WordPress
  and its plugins rather than to BioCytics, and it is not a product API.
- **BioDICE is not a public platform.** The BioDICE product page names openEHR, HL7, FHIR, OMOP and
  SMART-on-FHIR as data standards the system works with, but the platform is internal to BioCytics'
  own trials and services; none of those standards is exposed as a callable endpoint.
- Engagement is a sales conversation, not a signup: business development is reached at
  bizdev@biocytics.com, (980) 399-6810 or (704) 947-6599.

## Science and software

- [Science + Pipeline](https://biocytics.com/pipeline/): autologous adaptive immune cell therapy (AAICT) for solid tumors.
- [BioDICE](https://biocytics.com/biodice/): BioCytics Digitally Integrated Clinical Enterprise — an
  internal EMR / EDC-CDM / HAL lab documentation / CTMS suite with OmniQuery (natural-language search
  across openEHR, HL7, FHIR, OMOP, relational, CSV/JSON/XML sources) and OpenDICE (planned patient
  access to multi-institutional records via SMART-on-FHIR, SMART-on-openEHR and the Sequoia TEFCA
  network). Not offered as a hosted or callable product.
